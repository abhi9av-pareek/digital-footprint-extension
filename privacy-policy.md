# Digital Footprint Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Digital Footprint is a privacy-first browser extension designed to help users understand their browsing habits through local activity tracking, categories, history, and browsing streaks.

## 1. Our Privacy Commitment

Digital Footprint is designed so that your browsing activity stays on your device.

We do not operate a server for collecting Digital Footprint browsing data, and the extension does not send your browsing activity to us or to third-party analytics services.

No account is required to use the extension.

## 2. Information the Extension Processes

Digital Footprint processes limited information about websites you visit in order to provide its core functionality.

This may include:

- Website domain names, such as `github.com`
- Website names when they are recognized by the extension
- Website categories, such as Technology, Education, Entertainment, or Shopping
- Visit start and end times
- Visit duration
- Daily visit counts
- Daily category statistics
- Daily website statistics
- Streak information calculated from your local activity

Digital Footprint does not intentionally collect or store the full URL of a page.

For example, a URL such as:

`https://github.com/example/private-project/issues/123`

is reduced to the domain:

`github.com`

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

Digital Footprint does not intentionally collect:

- Passwords
- Cookies
- Form contents
- Page contents
- Text entered into websites
- Search queries
- Personal messages
- Files
- Screenshots
- Clipboard contents
- Precise location
- IP addresses
- Browsing history from outside the extension's own tracking system
- User account credentials

The extension does not use content scripts to read webpage content.

## 4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Digital Footprint stores its activity data locally using the browser's extension storage.

The data is stored on your device and is not uploaded to a Digital Footprint server.

The extension does not use:

- Cloud databases
- Analytics services
- Advertising trackers
- Third-party telemetry services
- Remote tracking servers

## 5. Why Website Access Is Required

Digital Footprint uses the browser's tab information to determine the domain of the currently active website.

This information is necessary for the extension's core functionality, including:

- Recording website visits
- Detecting when you switch websites
- Grouping activity into categories
- Calculating browsing streaks
- Displaying your local browsing history

The extension requests only the browser permissions required for this functionality.

## 6. Tracking Controls

You can pause website tracking from the extension's Settings screen.

When tracking is paused, Digital Footprint stops recording new website visits.

You can resume tracking at any time.

## 7. Deleting Your Data

Digital Footprint provides a "Clear All History" control in Settings.

Using this control deletes the browsing activity stored by the extension, including:

- Recorded visits
- Daily statistics
- Active tracking sessions

Clearing history does not affect your normal browser history or data stored by other browser extensions or websites.

## 8. Data Sharing

Digital Footprint does not sell, rent, or share your browsing activity with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Because Digital Footprint does not upload your browsing activity to a server, we do not have access to the browsing activity stored locally by your installation.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Digital Footprint does not use third-party analytics, advertising, telemetry, or cloud storage services to collect your browsing activity.

The extension's functionality is performed locally in your browser.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Digital Footprint does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the extension is designed to operate locally and does not require an account, it does not intentionally collect identifying information from users.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize the information processed by the extension.

The extension is designed around data minimization:

- Only website domains are used for website identification.
- Full URLs are not intentionally stored.
- Website activity is stored locally.
- No browsing activity is transmitted to a remote server.
- No analytics or advertising SDK is included.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of Digital Footprint change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those changes.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will also be updated.
